How to use this page. Your printed coursepack is the text; this page is the live layer beside it. Each row points to the StatLens tool or short guided activity that goes with a section. The section and example numbers here match your coursepack exactly. A ★ marks a guided activity that mirrors a specific coursepack example step by step; a plain (Ex …) tag means the link opens that example's data or setup directly. Either way, the example number matches your book. ★ activities walk you through a five-minute predict → do → explain; other links are open-ended tools for the topic. Everything works on your phone.
For instructors. Every link here is shareable and embeddable — drop any of them into Canvas, slides, or an assignment. For reproducible results in graded work, append a seed (e.g. ?seed=ch2demo). See the StatLens Instructor Hub.
