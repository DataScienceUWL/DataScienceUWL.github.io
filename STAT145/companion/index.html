How to use this page. Your printed coursepack is the text; this page is the live layer beside it. Each row points to the StatLens tool or short guided activity that goes with a section. The section and example numbers here match your coursepack exactly. A ★ marks a guided activity that mirrors a specific coursepack example step by step — the example number appears on the link (e.g. Ex 2.1.2). It opens the tool already set up and walks you through a five-minute predict → do → explain; the other links are open-ended tools for that topic. Everything works on your phone.
