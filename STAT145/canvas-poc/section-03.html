Association and Causation

Self Assess - Is this association or causation?

Daily exercise improves mental performance.

Which of the following best describes this type of claim?

Causation

Yes, the claim is that increasing exercises causes an increase in mental performance.

Association only

No, the claim is that increasing exercises causes an increase in mental performance.

Self Assess - Is this association or causation?

Cat owners tend to be more action educated than dog owners.

Which of the following best describes this type of claim?

Causation

No. There is no "action" verb here, rather there is just an observation about a relationship.

Association only

Yes. There is no "action" verb here, rather there is just an observation about a relationship.

Observational Studies vs Experiments

Observation or Experiment? - 1.3.1

Self Assess - Observational Study or Experiment?

To examine whether eating brown rice affects metabolism, we ask a random sample of people whether they eat brown rice and we also measure their metabolism rate.

Is this an observational study or an experiment?

Observational Study

Yes, there is no active treatment, just passive gathering of data.

Experiment

No, there is no active treatment, just passive gathering of data.

Self Assess - Observational Study or Experiment?

To examine whether playing music in a store increases the amount customers spend, we randomly assign some stores to play music and some to stay silent and compare the average amount spent by customers.

Is this an observational study or an experiment?

Observational Study

No, there is an active treatment - some stores get music and others do not.

Experiment

Yes, there is an active treatment - some stores get music and others do not.
