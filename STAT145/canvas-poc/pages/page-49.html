Self Assess - Computing the Mean and Median

The number of grams of fiber eaten in one day for a sample of ten people are

10 11 11 14 15 17 21 24 28 115

Compute the mean and median.

(Don’t enter all your work. Just do the work on paper and type in a number. We don’t actually check the number, but we do show you how the computation goes.)
The sum of the 10 observations is 266 so the mean is 266/10 or 26.6 grams.

The median is the average of the 5th and 6th numbers (in order) so (15 + 17)/2 = 16 grams.
