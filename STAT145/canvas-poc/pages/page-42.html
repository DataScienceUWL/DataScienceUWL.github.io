Self Assess - Proportions.

In a sample of 362 people there are 169 females and 193 males. 16 of the females are smokers and 27 of the males are smokers.

Make a two-way table to answer this question and two more.

What proportion of smokers are female? (Express your answer as a fraction like this: 37 / 96 )
There are 43 smokers and 16 of those are smokers, so 16 / 43.
