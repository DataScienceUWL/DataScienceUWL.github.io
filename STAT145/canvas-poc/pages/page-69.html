Self Assess - 1.5 IQR rule

The number of grams of fiber eaten in one day for a sample of ten people are

10 11 11 14 15 17 21 24 28 115

115 is pretty clearly an outlier, but apply the 1.5 IQR rule to “prove” that it is an outlier for this data. Start by computing the upper and lower fences.
lower fence = Q_1 - 1.5 IQR = 12.5 - 1.510 = 12.5 - 15 = -2.5.

upper fence = Q_3 + 1.5 IQR = 22.5 + 1.510 = 22.5 + 15 = 37.5

115 is far above the upper fence so it is definitely an outlier!
