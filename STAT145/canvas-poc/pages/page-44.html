Self Assess - Proportions.

In a sample of 362 people there are 169 females and 193 males. 16 of the females are smokers and 27 of the males are smokers.

What proportion of people in the sample are female and smokers? (Express your answer as a fraction like this: 37 / 96 )
We're considering the whole sample of 362 adults. Of those, there are 16 people who smoke and are female so the proportion is 16 / 362.
