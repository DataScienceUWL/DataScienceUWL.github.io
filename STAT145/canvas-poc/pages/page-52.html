Self Assess - Use the mean or the median?

The number of grams of fiber eaten in one day for a sample of ten people are

10 11 11 14 15 17 21 24 28 115

Is the mean or the median a better measure of center? Why?

Use the median. The mean is too high because of the extreme outlier value 115.
