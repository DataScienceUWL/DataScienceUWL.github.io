Self Assess - Is this a random sample?

The population is all people who visit the web-site CNN.com. All visitors to the website are invited to take part in the daily online poll.

True or False - this produces a random sample from the population.

True

No, this is voluntary samplling so we aren't getting a representative sample of all visitors to the site.

False

Good, this is voluntary samplling so we aren't getting a representative sample of all visitors to the site.

Self Assess - Is this biased?

Ask a random sample of students at the library on a Friday night “How many hours a week do you study?” to collect data to estimate the average number of hours a week that all college students study.

Is this sample biased? Yes or no.

Yes

Good. Students studying on Friday night probably don't represent all students.

No

No. Students studying on a Friday night at the library may be more extreme studiers than most and don't really represent all students.
