Measures of Center - Mean and Median

Self Assess - Computing the Mean and Median

The number of grams of fiber eaten in one day for a sample of ten people are

10 11 11 14 15 17 21 24 28 115

Compute the mean and median.

(Don’t enter all your work. Just do the work on paper and type in a number. We don’t actually check the number, but we do show you how the computation goes.)

Show Answer

The sum of the 10 observations is 266 so the mean is 266/10 or 26.6 grams.

The median is the average of the 5th and 6th numbers (in order) so (15 + 17)/2 = 16 grams.

Measures of Spread - IQR and Range

Ants Revisited - 2.2.3

Self Assess - Computing IQR

The number of grams of fiber eaten in one day for a sample of ten people are

10 11 11 14 15 17 21 24 28 115

Compute the IQR

Show Answer

Q_1 = ( 11 + 14 ) / 2 = 12.5

Q_3 = ( 21 + 24 ) / 2 = 22.5

IQR = Q_3 - Q_1 = 22.5 - 12.5 = 10 grams

Ants Revisited ... Again - 2.2.4

Self Assess - Computing Standard Deviation

The number of grams of fiber eaten in one day for a sample of ten people are

10 11 11 14 15 17 21 24 28 115

Compute the standard deviation.

Show Answer

First find the sum of the squared distances to the mean:

(10 - 26.6)^2 + (11 - 26.6)^2 + … = 9002.4

Now divide by n - 1 = 10 - 1 = 9 and then take the square root

9002.4 / 9 = 1000.267 —> take square root to get standard deviation = 31.6 grams.

Compute the z-score of the observation 11.

Show Answer
