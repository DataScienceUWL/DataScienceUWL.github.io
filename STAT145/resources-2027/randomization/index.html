Everything below is generated from one canonical chapter file. Change a number, fix a typo, or reword an example once, and every version here updates together — no drift between the book and what happens in class.

The textbook is the main learning source — the fullest version, with worked solutions you reveal after trying. The slides and packs are for in-class delivery. The two packs are the same chapter at different densities, so instructors can pick how much scaffolding they want.

How it works
