Textbook Structure

Part Chapters Topics
I. Explore 1–5 Data types, study design, visualization, distributions, relationships
II. Simulate 6–9 Sampling variability, randomization tests, bootstrap CIs, sampling distributions
III. Calculate 10–13 Normal approximation, t-based CIs and tests, inference for proportions
IV. Relationships 14–20 Chi-square, ANOVA, multiple comparisons, correlation, regression, prediction
V. Foundations 21–25 Probability rules, random variables, expected value, binomial, normal distribution
VI. Design 26–28 Type II error, statistical power, sample size planning

About

These materials support a simulation-based inference (SBI) approach to introductory statistics, following the pedagogical sequence: EDA → Simulation → Theory → Application. All materials are free for students.

The textbook remixes and augments content from Introduction to Modern Statistics (IMS) and OpenIntro Statistics, both published by the OpenIntro project, and is licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.

StatLens is a browser-based statistics toolkit built by Jeff Baggett to provide accessible simulation and exploration tools that integrate with the textbook via seeded URLs for reproducible assignments. The coursepack is developed at the University of Wisconsin-La Crosse.
