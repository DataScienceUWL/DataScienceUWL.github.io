Textbook Structure

Unit Chapters Topics
Unit 1. Explore 1–5 Data types, study design, categorical and quantitative displays, relationships
Unit 2. Simulate 6–10 Sampling variability, randomization tests, decision errors, bootstrap CIs, sampling distributions
Unit 3. Compute 11–14 Normal approximation, inference for proportions, t-based CIs and tests for means
Unit 4. Apply 15–23 Chi-square, ANOVA, multiple comparisons, correlation, regression, prediction, multiple regression
Unit 5. Foundations 24–31 Probability rules, random variables, expected value, binomial and normal distributions, power, sample size

31 numbered chapters in 5 units. Each unit also carries an unnumbered end-of-unit review, a Jamovi/StatLens technology tutorial, and (in most units) a data project. Five appendices cover technology setup, datasets, a StatLens quick reference, statistical tables, and solutions to odd-numbered exercises.

About

These materials support a simulation-based inference (SBI) approach to introductory statistics, following the pedagogical sequence: EDA → Simulation → Theory → Application. All materials are free for students.

The textbook remixes and augments content from Introduction to Modern Statistics (IMS) and OpenIntro Statistics, both published by the OpenIntro project, and is licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.

StatLens is a browser-based statistics toolkit built by Jeff Baggett to provide accessible simulation and exploration tools that integrate with the textbook via seeded URLs for reproducible assignments. The coursepack is developed at the University of Wisconsin-La Crosse.
