Problem 1: Line Integral with Respect to Arc Length

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_C yz \cos x \, ds\) where \(C\) is the curve parameterized by \(x = t\), \(y = 3\cos t\), \(z = 3\sin t\) for \(0 \le t \le \pi\).

Problem 2: Line Integral Using Green's Theorem

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_C \sqrt{1 + x^3} \, dx + 2xy \, dy\) where \(C\) is the counter-clockwise boundary of the triangle with vertices \((0, 0)\), \((1, 0)\), and \((1, 3)\). There's an easy way and a hard way...

Problem 3: Conservative Vector Fields and FTOLI

For \(\mathbf{F} = \langle e^y, xe^y + e^z, ye^z \rangle\):

  • Show that \(\mathbf{F}\) is conservative (without constructing a potential function).
  • Find a potential function for \(\mathbf{F}\).
  • Use the potential function to evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r}\) where \(C\) is the line segment from \((0, 2, 0)\) to \((4, 0, 3)\).
