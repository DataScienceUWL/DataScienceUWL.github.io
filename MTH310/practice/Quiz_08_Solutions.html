Problem 1: Triple Integral Between Parabolic Cylinders

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\iiint_E xy \, dV\) where \(E\) is bounded by the parabolic cylinders \(y = x^2\) and \(x = y^2\) and the planes \(z = 0\) and \(z = x + y\).

Solution:

Setup: The region in the \(xy\)-plane is bounded by \(y = x^2\) (below) and \(y = \sqrt{x}\) (above), for \(0 \le x \le 1\).

\[\iiint_E xy \, dV = \int_0^1 \int_{x^2}^{\sqrt{x}} \int_0^{x+y} xy \, dz \, dy \, dx\]

Step 1: Integrate with respect to \(z\).

\[= \int_0^1 \int_{x^2}^{\sqrt{x}} xy(x + y) \, dy \, dx = \int_0^1 \int_{x^2}^{\sqrt{x}} (x^2 y + xy^2) \, dy \, dx\]

Step 2: Integrate with respect to \(y\).

\[= \int_0^1 \left[\frac{x^2 y^2}{2} + \frac{xy^3}{3}\right]_{y=x^2}^{y=\sqrt{x}} dx\]

\[= \int_0^1 \left(\frac{x^2 \cdot x}{2} + \frac{x \cdot x^{3/2}}{3} - \frac{x^2 \cdot x^4}{2} - \frac{x \cdot x^6}{3}\right) dx\]

\[= \int_0^1 \left(\frac{x^3}{2} + \frac{x^{5/2}}{3} - \frac{x^6}{2} - \frac{x^7}{3}\right) dx\]

Step 3: Integrate with respect to \(x\).

\[= \left[\frac{x^4}{8} + \frac{2x^{7/2}}{21} - \frac{x^7}{14} - \frac{x^8}{24}\right]_0^1\]

\[= \frac{1}{8} + \frac{2}{21} - \frac{1}{14} - \frac{1}{24}\]

Step 4: Find common denominator (168).

\[= \frac{21 + 16 - 12 - 7}{168} = \frac{18}{168} = \boxed{\frac{3}{28}}\]

Problem 2: Triple Integral in Spherical Coordinates (Setup Only)

Set up, but do not evaluate, \(\displaystyle\iiint_H z^3\sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2} \, dV\), where \(H\) is between the spheres \(x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1\) and \(x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 4\), above the plane \(z = 0\), and between the planes \(y = x\) and \(y = \sqrt{3}x\).

Solution:

Setup: Convert to spherical coordinates:

  • \(x = \rho \sin\phi \cos\theta\)
  • \(y = \rho \sin\phi \sin\theta\)
  • \(z = \rho \cos\phi\)
  • \(dV = \rho^2 \sin\phi \, d\rho \, d\phi \, d\theta\)

Determine bounds:

  • \(\rho\): from 1 to 2 (between the two spheres)
  • \(\phi\): from 0 to \(\frac{\pi}{2}\) (above \(z = 0\))
  • \(\theta\): \(y = x \implies \theta = \frac{\pi}{4}\); \(y = \sqrt{3}x \implies \theta = \frac{\pi}{3}\)

Convert the integrand:

\[z^3 = (\rho\cos\phi)^3 = \rho^3\cos^3\phi\]

\[\sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2} = \rho\]

So the integrand becomes: \(\rho^3\cos^3\phi \cdot \rho = \rho^4\cos^3\phi\)

Final Answer:

\[\boxed{\int_{\pi/4}^{\pi/3} \int_0^{\pi/2} \int_1^2 \rho^4 \cos^3\phi \cdot \rho^2 \sin\phi \, d\rho \, d\phi \, d\theta = \int_{\pi/4}^{\pi/3} \int_0^{\pi/2} \int_1^2 \rho^6 \cos^3\phi \sin\phi \, d\rho \, d\phi \, d\theta}\]

Problem 3: Triple Integral with Cylindrical Coordinates

Set up and evaluate \(\displaystyle\iiint_E z \, dV\), where \(E\) is bounded by the planes \(y = 0\), \(z = 0\), \(x + y = 2\) and the cylinder \(y^2 + z^2 = 1\) in the first octant.

Solution:

Setup: Use cylindrical coordinates in \(y\) and \(z\): \(y = r\cos\theta\), \(z = r\sin\theta\), \(x = x\).

Bounds: \(0 \le \theta \le \frac{\pi}{2}\), \(0 \le r \le 1\), \(0 \le x \le 2 - r\cos\theta\).

\[\iiint_E z \, dV = \int_0^{\pi/2} \int_0^1 \int_0^{2-r\cos\theta} (r\sin\theta) \cdot r \, dx \, dr \, d\theta\]

Step 1: Integrate with respect to \(x\).

\[= \int_0^{\pi/2} \int_0^1 r^2 \sin\theta (2 - r\cos\theta) \, dr \, d\theta\]

\[= \int_0^{\pi/2} \int_0^1 (2r^2\sin\theta - r^3\sin\theta\cos\theta) \, dr \, d\theta\]

Step 2: Integrate with respect to \(r\).

\[= \int_0^{\pi/2} \left[\frac{2r^3}{3}\sin\theta - \frac{r^4}{4}\sin\theta\cos\theta\right]_0^1 d\theta\]

\[= \int_0^{\pi/2} \left(\frac{2}{3}\sin\theta - \frac{1}{4}\sin\theta\cos\theta\right) d\theta\]

\[= \int_0^{\pi/2} \left(\frac{2}{3}\sin\theta - \frac{1}{8}\sin 2\theta\right) d\theta\]

Step 3: Integrate with respect to \(\theta\).

\[= \left[-\frac{2}{3}\cos\theta + \frac{1}{16}\cos 2\theta\right]_0^{\pi/2}\]

\[= \left(-\frac{2}{3}\cos\frac{\pi}{2} + \frac{1}{16}\cos\pi\right) - \left(-\frac{2}{3}\cos 0 + \frac{1}{16}\cos 0\right)\]

\[= \left(0 - \frac{1}{16}\right) - \left(-\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{16}\right) = -\frac{1}{16} + \frac{2}{3} - \frac{1}{16}\]

\[= \frac{2}{3} - \frac{2}{16} = \frac{2}{3} - \frac{1}{8} = \frac{16 - 3}{24} = \boxed{\frac{13}{24}}\]
