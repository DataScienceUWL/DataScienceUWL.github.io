MTH 310: Calculus III

Fall 2024

You may use a calculator.

Problem 1: Linear Approximation

Find the linear approximation of \(f(x, y) = 1 - xy\cos(\pi y)\) at \((1, 1)\) and use it to approximate \(f(1.02, 0.97)\).

Problem 2: Chain Rule

\(w = x^2 + y^2 + z^2\), where \(x = st\), \(y = s\cos t\), \(z = s\sin t\).

Find \(\dfrac{\partial w}{\partial s}\) at \((s, t) = (4, 0)\). Use the chain rule.

Problem 3: Tangent Plane

For the surface \(y = x^2 - z^2\) at the point \((4, 7, 3)\), find the equation of the tangent plane.

Problem 4: Directional Derivative and Gradient

Consider the function \(f(x, y) = \dfrac{x}{x^2 + y^2}\).

  • Find the directional derivative at \((1, 2)\) moving toward \((-2, 6)\).
  • Find the maximum rate of change of \(f\) at the point \((1, 2)\) and the direction in which it occurs.
Problem 5: Differentials and Error Estimation

Three positive numbers, each less than 50, are rounded to the first decimal place (e.g., 14.078 becomes 14.1) and then multiplied together. Use differentials to estimate the maximum possible error in the computed product that might result from rounding.
