Ellipsoid: A 3D surface plot of the equation x²/4 + y²/2.25 + z²/1 = 1.
The surface is a smooth, closed, egg-shaped surface centered at the origin.
It extends from x = -2 to x = 2 along the x-axis (longest direction),
from y = -1.5 to y = 1.5 along the y-axis,
and from z = -1 to z = 1 along the z-axis (shortest direction).
All cross-sections parallel to the coordinate planes are ellipses.
