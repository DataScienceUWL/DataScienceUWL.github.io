Hyperboloid of Two Sheets: A 3D surface plot showing the equation -x² - y² + z² = 1.
The surface consists of two separate bowl-shaped sheets that never touch.
The upper sheet has its vertex (a single point) at z = 1 on the z-axis and opens upward,
growing wider as z increases. The lower sheet has its vertex at z = -1 and opens downward.
There is a gap between z = -1 and z = 1 where no surface exists.
Horizontal cross-sections (z = constant with |z| > 1) are circles centered on the z-axis,
with radius √(z² - 1). Vertical cross-sections through the z-axis are hyperbolas opening
in the ±z directions.
