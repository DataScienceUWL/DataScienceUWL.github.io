Spherical Wedge Region: A 3D plot showing a wedge-shaped region in spherical coordinates.
The region is bounded by:
- Two concentric spherical shells at ρ = 1 (inner, lighter purple) and ρ = 2 (outer, darker purple)
- The xy-plane (z = 0) as the bottom (green), restricting to the upper hemisphere (φ from 0 to π/2)
- Two vertical half-planes: y = x (θ = π/4) and y = √3·x (θ = π/3), shown in orange
The wedge extends from the z-axis outward, spanning a 15-degree angular range (π/12 radians)
in the azimuthal direction. This is the region of integration for the spherical coordinate integral.
