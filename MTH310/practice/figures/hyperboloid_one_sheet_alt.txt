Hyperboloid of One Sheet: A 3D surface plot of the equation x² + y² - z²/2.25 = 1.
The surface is a single connected piece that looks like an hourglass or cooling tower.
It is narrowest at z = 0 (the "waist"), where the cross-section is a circle of radius 1.
As z increases or decreases from 0, the surface flares outward.
Horizontal cross-sections (z = constant) are circles of radius √(1 + z²/2.25).
Vertical cross-sections through the z-axis are hyperbolas opening outward.
