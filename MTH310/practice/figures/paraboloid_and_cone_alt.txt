Paraboloid and Cone - Bounded Region: A 3D plot showing the region between two surfaces
from the origin (z=0) to their intersection at z=1.
The blue surface is the paraboloid z = r² (or z = x² + y²), which curves upward from the origin.
The orange surface is the cone z = r (or z = √(x² + y²)), which rises linearly from the origin.
Both surfaces meet at the origin and intersect along a circle at r = 1, z = 1.
The cone lies above the paraboloid throughout the region 0 < r < 1.
The shaded region between them (cone above, paraboloid below) represents the volume
to be calculated using the integral in polar coordinates.
