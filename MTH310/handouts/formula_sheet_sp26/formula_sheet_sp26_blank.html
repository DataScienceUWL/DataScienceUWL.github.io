Dot Product

\(\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = a_1b_1 + a_2b_2 + a_3b_3 = |\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta\)

Angle: \(\cos\theta = \dfrac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}}{|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|}\)

Scalar projection: \(\text{comp}_{\vec{a}}\vec{b} = \dfrac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}}{|\vec{a}|}\)

Vector projection: \(\text{proj}_{\vec{a}}\vec{b} = \dfrac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}}{|\vec{a}|^2}\,\vec{a}\)

Cross Product

\(\vec{a}\times\vec{b} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ a_1 & a_2 & a_3 \\ b_1 & b_2 & b_3 \end{vmatrix}\)

\(|\vec{a}\times\vec{b}| = |\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\sin\theta\)

Area of parallelogram \(= |\vec{a}\times\vec{b}|\)

Area of triangle \(= \tfrac{1}{2}|\vec{a}\times\vec{b}|\)

Lines & Planes

Line through \(P_0\) with direction \(\vec{v} = \langle a,b,c\rangle\): \[\begin{aligned} \vec{r} &= \vec{r}_0 + t\vec{v} \\ x &= x_0 + at,\; y = y_0 + bt,\; z = z_0 + ct \end{aligned}\]

Plane with normal \(\vec{n} = \langle a,b,c\rangle\): \[a(x-x_0) + b(y-y_0) + c(z-z_0) = 0\]

Distance from point to plane: \[D = \frac{|ax_1 + by_1 + cz_1 - d|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}}\]

Vector Functions (Ch. 13)

\(\vec{r}(t) = \langle f(t),\, g(t),\, h(t)\rangle\)

Derivative: \(\vec{r}\,'(t) = \langle f'(t),\, g'(t),\, h'(t)\rangle\)

Unit tangent: \(\vec{T}(t) = \dfrac{\vec{r}\,'(t)}{|\vec{r}\,'(t)|}\)

Arc length: \(L = \displaystyle\int_a^b |\vec{r}\,'(t)|\,dt\)

Curvature: \(\kappa = \dfrac{|\vec{r}\,'\times\vec{r}\,''|}{|\vec{r}\,'|^3}\)

Partial Derivatives (Ch. 14)

Tangent plane at \((x_0, y_0, z_0)\): \[z - z_0 = f_x(x_0,y_0)(x-x_0) + f_y(x_0,y_0)(y-y_0)\]

Linearization: \[L = f(a,b) + f_x(a,b)(x{-}a) + f_y(a,b)(y{-}b)\]

Total differential: \(dz = f_x\,dx + f_y\,dy\)

Chain Rule

If \(z = f(x,y)\), \(x = x(t)\), \(y = y(t)\): \[\frac{dz}{dt} = \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\frac{dx}{dt} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\frac{dy}{dt}\]

If \(x = x(s,t)\), \(y = y(s,t)\): \[\frac{\partial z}{\partial s} = f_x\frac{\partial x}{\partial s} + f_y\frac{\partial y}{\partial s}\]

Implicit: If \(F(x,y,z) = 0\): \[\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = -\frac{F_x}{F_z}, \qquad \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = -\frac{F_y}{F_z}\]

Directional Derivative & Gradient

\(\nabla f = \langle f_x,\, f_y \rangle\) (or \(\langle f_x, f_y, f_z\rangle\) in 3D)

\[D_{\vec{u}}f = \nabla f \cdot \vec{u} \qquad (\vec{u} \text{ is a unit vector})\]

Max rate of change \(= |\nabla f|\), in direction \(\nabla f\)

\(\nabla f\) is \(\perp\) to level curves/surfaces.

Second Derivative Test

At a critical point \((a,b)\) where \(f_x = f_y = 0\):

\[D = f_{xx}\,f_{yy} - (f_{xy})^2\]

\(D > 0\), \(f_{xx} > 0\) local min
\(D > 0\), \(f_{xx} < 0\) local max
\(D < 0\) saddle point
\(D = 0\) inconclusive

Lagrange Multipliers

Optimize \(f\) subject to \(g(x,y) = k\): \[\nabla f = \lambda\,\nabla g \qquad \text{and} \qquad g = k\]

System: \(f_x = \lambda g_x\), \(f_y = \lambda g_y\), \(g = k\)

Trig Identities

Pythagorean: \[\begin{gathered} \sin^2\!\theta + \cos^2\!\theta = 1 \\ 1 + \tan^2\!\theta = \sec^2\!\theta \\ 1 + \cot^2\!\theta = \csc^2\!\theta \end{gathered}\]

Half-Angle (Power Reduction): \[\begin{aligned} \sin^2\!\theta &= \tfrac{1}{2}(1 - \cos 2\theta) \\ \cos^2\!\theta &= \tfrac{1}{2}(1 + \cos 2\theta) \end{aligned}\]

Double-Angle: \[\begin{aligned} \sin 2\theta &= 2\sin\theta\cos\theta \\ \cos 2\theta &= \cos^2\!\theta - \sin^2\!\theta \end{aligned}\]

Integration Formulas

\[\begin{aligned} \int \sec\theta\,d\theta &= \ln|\sec\theta + \tan\theta| + C \\ \int \csc\theta\,d\theta &= -\ln|\csc\theta + \cot\theta| + C \\ \int \tan\theta\,d\theta &= \ln|\sec\theta| + C \\ \int \frac{du}{a^2 + u^2} &= \frac{1}{a}\arctan\!\frac{u}{a} + C \\ \int \frac{du}{\sqrt{a^2 - u^2}} &= \arcsin\!\frac{u}{a} + C \end{aligned}\]

Trig powers: \[\begin{aligned} \int \sin^2\!\theta\,d\theta &= \frac{\theta}{2} - \frac{\sin 2\theta}{4} + C \\ \int \cos^2\!\theta\,d\theta &= \frac{\theta}{2} + \frac{\sin 2\theta}{4} + C \\ \int \sec^3\!\theta\,d\theta &= \tfrac{1}{2}\!\big(\sec\theta\tan\theta \\[-2pt] &\quad + \ln|\sec\theta{+}\tan\theta|\big) + C \end{aligned}\]

Trig Substitution

See Let Identity
\(\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}\) \(x = a\sin\theta\) \(1 - \sin^2 = \cos^2\)
\(\sqrt{a^2 + x^2}\) \(x = a\tan\theta\) \(1 + \tan^2 = \sec^2\)
\(\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}\) \(x = a\sec\theta\) \(\sec^2 - 1 = \tan^2\)

Integration by Parts

\[\int u\,dv = uv - \int v\,du\] LIATE priority: Logs, Inverse trig, Algebraic, Trig, Exponential.

Coordinate Systems

Polar (\(r, \theta\)): \[\begin{aligned} x &= r\cos\theta, \quad y = r\sin\theta \\ r^2 &= x^2 + y^2, \quad dA = r\,dr\,d\theta \end{aligned}\]

Cylindrical (\(r, \theta, z\)): \[\begin{aligned} x &= r\cos\theta, \; y = r\sin\theta, \; z = z \\ dV &= r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta \end{aligned}\]

Spherical (\(\rho, \phi, \theta\)): \[\begin{aligned} x &= \rho\sin\phi\cos\theta \\ y &= \rho\sin\phi\sin\theta \\ z &= \rho\cos\phi \\ \rho^2 &= x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \\ dV &= \rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta \end{aligned}\] \(\phi\in[0,\pi]\) from \(+z\)-axis, \(\theta\in[0,2\pi]\)

Multiple Integrals

Surface area of \(z = f(x,y)\): \[A = \iint_D \sqrt{1 + f_x^2 + f_y^2}\,dA\]

Center of mass (\(\rho\) = density): \[\bar{x} = \frac{\iint_D x\,\rho\,dA}{\iint_D \rho\,dA}, \qquad \bar{y} = \frac{\iint_D y\,\rho\,dA}{\iint_D \rho\,dA}\]

Vector Calc Operations

\(\vec{F} = \langle P, Q, R \rangle\)

Gradient: \(\nabla f = \langle f_x,\, f_y,\, f_z \rangle\)

Divergence: \(\nabla \cdot \vec{F} = P_x + Q_y + R_z\)

Curl: \[\nabla \times \vec{F} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ \partial_x & \partial_y & \partial_z \\ P & Q & R \end{vmatrix}\] \[= \langle R_y - Q_z,\; P_z - R_x,\; Q_x - P_y \rangle\]

Conservative test: \(\nabla \times \vec{F} = \vec{0}\)

Line Integrals

Arc length: \(\displaystyle\int_C ds = \int_a^b \|\vec{r}\,'(t)\|\,dt\)

Work: \(\displaystyle\int_C \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = \int_a^b \vec{F}(\vec{r}(t)) \cdot \vec{r}\,'(t)\,dt\)

Fund. Thm. of Line Integrals:

If \(\vec{F} = \nabla f\): \(\displaystyle\int_C \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r} = f(B) - f(A)\)

Surface Integrals

Flux through \(z = f(x,y)\) (upward normal): \[\iint_S \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{S} = \iint_D \vec{F} \cdot \langle -f_x, -f_y, 1 \rangle\,dA\]

Parametric surface: \(d\vec{S} = (\vec{r}_u \times \vec{r}_v)\,du\,dv\)

Surface area (parametric): \[A = \iint_D \|\vec{r}_u \times \vec{r}_v\|\,du\,dv\]

The Big Three Theorems

Green’s Theorem (2D, CCW): \[\oint_C P\,dx + Q\,dy = \iint_D \!\left(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial P}{\partial y}\right)dA\]

Stokes’ Theorem (surface \(\to\) boundary): \[\iint_S (\nabla \times \vec{F}) \cdot d\vec{S} = \oint_C \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r}\]

Divergence Theorem (solid \(\to\) boundary): \[\iiint_E (\nabla \cdot \vec{F})\,dV = \oiint_S \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{S}\]
