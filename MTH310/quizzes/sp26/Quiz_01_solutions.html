Question 1. 3 pts

Use the figure below to answer the questions that follow. The vectors \(\mathbf{u}\) and \(\mathbf{v}\) lie in the plane of the page.

  1. Find \(\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}\).

    \(\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = |\mathbf{u}|\,|\mathbf{v}|\cos\theta = (2)(3)\cos 45^\circ = 6 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = 3\sqrt{2}.\)

  2. Find \(|\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}|\).

    \(|\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}| = |\mathbf{u}|\,|\mathbf{v}|\sin\theta = (2)(3)\sin 45^\circ = 6 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = 3\sqrt{2}.\)

  3. Is \(\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}\) directed into the page or out of the page?

    By the right-hand rule, curling from \(\mathbf{u}\) toward \(\mathbf{v}\) (counterclockwise), \(\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}\) points out of the page.

Question 2. 4 pts

Find two unit vectors that are orthogonal to both \(\mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{i} - 2\mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}\).

Let \(\mathbf{a}=\langle 0,1,2\rangle\) and \(\mathbf{b}=\langle 1,-2,3\rangle\). Then \[\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \\ 1 & -2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = \langle 7, 2, -1 \rangle.\] \(|\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}| = \sqrt{54} = 3\sqrt{6}\). The two unit vectors are \(\displaystyle\pm\,\frac{1}{3\sqrt{6}}\langle 7, 2, -1\rangle.\)

Question 3. 4 pts

Find the vector projection of \(\mathbf{b} = \langle 12, 1, 2 \rangle\) onto \(\mathbf{a} = \langle -1, 4, 8 \rangle\).

\(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = -12+4+16 = 8\), \(|\mathbf{a}|^2 = 1+16+64 = 81\).

\(\displaystyle\text{proj}_{\mathbf{a}}\mathbf{b} = \frac{8}{81}\langle -1,4,8\rangle = \left\langle \frac{-8}{81},\, \frac{32}{81},\, \frac{64}{81}\right\rangle.\)

Question 4. 4 pts

Find the angle at vertex \(B\), to the nearest tenth of a degree, for the triangle with vertices \(A(1,0,-1)\), \(B(3,-2,0)\), and \(C(1,3,3)\).

\(\overrightarrow{BA} = \langle -2,2,-1\rangle\), \(\overrightarrow{BC} = \langle -2,5,3\rangle\).

\(\overrightarrow{BA}\cdot\overrightarrow{BC} = 4+10-3 = 11\).

\(|\overrightarrow{BA}| = 3\), \(|\overrightarrow{BC}| = \sqrt{38}\).

\(\displaystyle\cos\angle B = \frac{11}{3\sqrt{38}}\), \(\angle B \approx 53.6^\circ\).
