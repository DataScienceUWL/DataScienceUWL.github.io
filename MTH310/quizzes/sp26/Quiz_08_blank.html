Quiz 8 MTH 310

Sections 15.7, 15.8, 16.1, 16.2

Question 1 [4 pts]

Evaluate the integral \(\iiint_E z\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\, dV\), where \(E\) is the solid inside the cylinder \(x^2 + y^2 = 1\), between \(z = 0\) and the cone \(z = \sqrt{x^2+y^2}\).

Question 2 [4 pts]

Evaluate the integral \(\iiint_E z\, dV\), where \(E\) is the region inside the sphere \(x^2+y^2+z^2 = 4\) and above the cone \(\phi = \pi/3\).

Question 3 [3 pts]

(a) (2 pts) Compute \(\nabla f\) for \(f(x,y) = xe^y + y^2\).

(b) (1 pts) Using part (a), explain why the vector field \(\mathbf{F}(x,y) = \langle e^y,\; xe^y + 2y \rangle\) is conservative.

Question 4 [4 pts]

Evaluate the line integral \(\int_C \mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{r}\), where \(\mathbf{F}(x,y) = \langle x+y,\; y \rangle\) and \(C\) is the line segment from \((1,0)\) to \((3,2)\).
