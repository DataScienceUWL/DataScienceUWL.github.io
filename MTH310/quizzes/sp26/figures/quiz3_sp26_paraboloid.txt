Three-dimensional plot of the elliptic paraboloid z = 4 - x squared - 4y squared. The surface is a bowl opening downward with its vertex at (0, 0, 4). A red curve shows the xz-trace (y=0), a downward parabola z = 4 - x squared with x-intercepts at plus or minus 2. A green curve shows the yz-trace (x=0), a narrower downward parabola z = 4 - 4y squared with y-intercepts at plus or minus 1. Dashed ellipses show horizontal traces at z = 0 (larger, semi-axes 2 and 1) and z = 2 (smaller, semi-axes sqrt(2) and sqrt(2)/2).
