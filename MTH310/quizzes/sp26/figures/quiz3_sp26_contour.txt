Contour map (top-down view) of z = 4 - x squared - 4y squared in the xy-plane. Three level curves are shown as concentric ellipses centered at the origin. The outermost ellipse (purple, labeled z=0) has semi-axes a=2 along x and b=1 along y. The middle ellipse (amber, labeled z=2) has semi-axes a=sqrt(2) along x and b=sqrt(2)/2 along y. The innermost level curve (red dot, labeled z=4) is just the origin, representing the vertex of the paraboloid viewed from above. The ellipses shrink inward as z increases toward 4.
