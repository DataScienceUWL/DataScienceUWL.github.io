Question 1 [8 pts]

A grain silo consists of a cylinder of radius \(r = 3\) m and height \(h = 8\) m topped by a hemisphere of radius \(r = 3\) m. The silo is built from wooden planks that are \(0.05\) m thick.

  1. (4 pts) The volume of the silo is \(V = \pi r^2 h + \dfrac{2}{3}\pi r^3\). Find the total differential \(dV\).

    \[dV = \frac{\partial V}{\partial r}\, dr + \frac{\partial V}{\partial h}\, dh = (2\pi r h + 2\pi r^2)\, dr + \pi r^2\, dh\]

  2. (4 pts) Determine the appropriate values of \(dr\) and \(dh\) for this problem, then use differentials to estimate the total volume of wood in the silo walls, roof, and floor.

    The planks add one layer outward on the cylindrical walls and the dome, so \(dr = 0.05\).

    The silo has a flat wooden floor (bottom) but no flat top — the hemisphere replaces the lid. So only the floor adds to the height: \(dh = 0.05\).

    \[\begin{aligned} dV &= (2\pi(3)(8) + 2\pi(9))(0.05) + \pi(9)(0.05) \\ &= (48\pi + 18\pi)(0.05) + 0.45\pi \\ &= 3.3\pi + 0.45\pi \\ &= \boxed{3.75\pi \approx 11.78 \text{ m}^3} \end{aligned}\]

Question 2 [7 pts]

Let \(w = xy + yz + xz\), where \(x = s^2 + t\), \(y = st\), and \(z = t^2\).

  1. (3 pts) Draw the tree diagram and write the chain rule expression for \(\dfrac{\partial w}{\partial t}\).

    Tree diagram: \(w\) depends on \(x, y, z\), each of which depends on \(s\) and \(t\).

    \[\frac{\partial w}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial w}{\partial x}\frac{\partial x}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial w}{\partial y}\frac{\partial y}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial w}{\partial z}\frac{\partial z}{\partial t}\]

  2. (4 pts) Evaluate \(\dfrac{\partial w}{\partial t}\) at \((s, t) = (1, 2)\).

    At \((s, t) = (1, 2)\): \(x = 1 + 2 = 3\), \(y = 2\), \(z = 4\).

    Partials of \(w\): \[\frac{\partial w}{\partial x} = y + z = 6, \qquad \frac{\partial w}{\partial y} = x + z = 7, \qquad \frac{\partial w}{\partial z} = x + y = 5\]

    Partials of \(x, y, z\) with respect to \(t\): \[\frac{\partial x}{\partial t} = 1, \qquad \frac{\partial y}{\partial t} = s = 1, \qquad \frac{\partial z}{\partial t} = 2t = 4\]

    Therefore: \[\frac{\partial w}{\partial t} = 6(1) + 7(1) + 5(4) = 6 + 7 + 20 = \boxed{33}\]
