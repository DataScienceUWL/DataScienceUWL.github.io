Quiz 3 MTH 310

Sections 12.6, 13.1--13.2

Question 1. 9 pts

Consider the surface \(z = 4 - x^2 - 4y^2\).

  1. Find the horizontal traces (\(z = k\)). For what values of \(k\) do real traces exist?

  2. Find the vertical traces in the \(xz\)-plane (\(y = 0\)) and the \(yz\)-plane (\(x = 0\)).

  3. Sketch the surface. Label the vertex and at least one trace in each direction.

  4. Sketch a contour map (in the \(xy\)-plane) showing the level curves for \(z = 0\), \(z = 2\), and \(z = 4\). Label each curve with its \(z\)-value.

Question 2. 6 pts

Consider the helix \(\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle 3\cos t,\, 3\sin t,\, 4t \rangle\).

  1. Find the first point (with \(t > 0\)) where the curve pierces the \(xz\)-plane.

  2. Find the unit tangent vector \(\mathbf{T}\) at the point from (a).

  3. Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2} \mathbf{r}(t)\, dt\).
