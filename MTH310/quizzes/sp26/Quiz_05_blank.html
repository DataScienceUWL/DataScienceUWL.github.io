Quiz 5 MTH 310

Sections 14.6--14.7

Question 1 [5 pts]

Let \(f(x,y) = x^2 y + \sqrt{y}\).

  1. (3 pts) Find the directional derivative of \(f\) at the point \((2, 1)\) in the direction toward the point \((5, 5)\).

  2. (2 pts) What is the minimum rate of change of \(f\) at \((2, 1)\), and in what direction does it occur?

Question 2 [4 pts]

Find all critical points of \(f(x,y) = x^3 - 6xy + 8y^3\) and classify each as a local maximum, local minimum, or saddle point.

Question 3 [6 pts]

Find the absolute maximum and absolute minimum values of \(f(x,y) = x^2 - 2x + y^2\) on the closed disk \(x^2 + y^2 \le 4\).
